ABOUT ROAD TRANSPORTATION

Dependable Road Transportation for Your Growing Business

RB Connect Global Network provides dependable road transportation services for domestic and cross-border cargo movement. Our network-driven road logistics are designed to meet your supply chain requirements with speed, safety, competitive pricing, and complete transparency.

From Full Truck Load (FTL) and Part Truck Load (PTL) to container drayage, express dispatches, industrial cargo movements, and last-mile delivery, we provide tailored road freight solutions adapted to your exact timeline and budget.

ROAD FREIGHT SOLUTIONS

Dependable Road Transportation

DEDICATED FLEET

Full Truck Load (FTL)

Dedicated point-to-point vehicle dispatch for high-volume commercial consignments with direct delivery routes.

SHARED CAPACITY

Part Truck Load (PTL)

Cost-effective shared transit options for mid-sized freight, paying only for the truck space your cargo occupies.

INTERMODAL TRANSIT

Container Transportation

Intermodal drayage linking major container terminals, railheads, inland depots, and industrial production plants.

HIGH VELOCITY

Express Transportation

Time-critical road freight logistics with non-stop driver teams and real-time GPS telemetry for emergency shipments.

PRECISION DISPATCH

Last-Mile Delivery

Precision terminal-to-door distribution guaranteeing secure recipient receipt with digital proof-of-delivery (POD).

HEAVY-DUTY LOGISTICS

Industrial Cargo Transportation

Heavy-duty fleet solutions for raw materials, machinery, engineering parts, and bulk industrial manufacturing supplies.

CONTRACTED TRANSPORT
